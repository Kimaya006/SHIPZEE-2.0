How Shipzee works

  1. 1

    A driver posts a trip

    Say where you are going, when you leave and how much space you can spare.

  2. 2

    A sender picks a driver

    Search your route, compare drivers and rates, and send a request with the parcel's weight.

  3. 3

    The parcel arrives

    Pickup and delivery are each confirmed with a one time code, so the right parcel reaches the right person.

For senders

  • Search trips by route and date
  • See the driver, departure time and rate per kg before you decide
  • Pay by weight, not by vehicle

For drivers

  • Post a trip in under a minute
  • Set your own rate per kg and the space you can spare
  • Cancel a trip any time before you leave

Shipzee is a student project. Payments are simulated and some features are still being built.
